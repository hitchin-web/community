# Hitchin Web Code of Conduct

Our guiding principle is simple: **we are for everyone**. By attending a Hitchin Web event or engaging online you agree to keep the space friendly, inclusive, and harassment‑free.

1. **Respect everyone.** Discrimination, harassment, aggression, or demeaning language have no place here.
1. **Keep it non‑commercial.** No unsolicited sales, recruitment, or spam.
1. **Mind the space and those around us.** Look after the venue, be considerate to staff and neighbours, and keep noise and mess to a minimum.
1. **Drink responsibly (or not at all).** No‑one should feel pressured to consume alcohol and intoxicated behaviour will be challenged.
1. **See something? Say something.** If anything makes you or someone else uncomfortable, tell an organiser immediately (via Meetup, WhatsApp, or in person). We will act quickly, discreetly, and may ask anyone to modify their behaviour or leave.

These guidelines are a living document; we’ll review them periodically to keep Hitchin Web welcoming for all.
